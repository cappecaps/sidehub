> ...and to be really sure you need to have a doubt

Hello! Welcome to this blog... or whatever this is. I created it with the intent to collect all my "side projects", publish them in a single place, and making them available in the internet.

For reasons outside my comprehension, I am obsessed with creating mathematical models serving the purpose to describe the world around me, explore ideas, or solve doubts that pop up in my head. These models must rigorously be _ab initio_, namely from first principles. My end goal is, whether possible, to derive an elegant - analytics - equation that ultimately describes the system in question. Modeling is a challenge that necessitates perseverance, but at the same time it stimulates deep thinking and sharpen problem solving skills. Carefully choosing the approximations is in itself a real form of art: it requires creativity and imagination, as well as the skill to visualize your system and what you want to narrate through the model.

Nothing of what I've done is really new, quite the opposite: I am obsessed with withdrawing in my shell and not looking anywhere for hints or solutions, except when strictly necessary. Most of the concepts I write here are surely well-known since the 1800, and many interpretations may be inaccurate at best. This blog is not meant to be an enciclopedia, rather a collection of my thoughts, ideas, and obsessions. If I ever catch the interest of even one person in the world, I'll be more than happy.

Please feel free to contact me for any feedback, suggestions, or corrections! 

**Marco**
